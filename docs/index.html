How to read this page

A successful processing job does not mean a passing KPI. A daily KPI requires a passing thematic metric on a distinct date; hourly products require at least one passing validated hour. The monthly target is five dates with at least one in each North, Center and South sub-AOI. Monthly products use a reference-supported month. Product 04's uncertainty-adjusted result is a theoretical lower bound, shown separately from contractual acceptance. Counts cover uploaded packages with hosted results, not every production date.
